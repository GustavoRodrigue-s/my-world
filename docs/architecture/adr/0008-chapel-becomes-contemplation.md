# 0008 — The Capela becomes a place of contemplation

- **Status:** accepted (not implemented)
- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Supersedes:** the Capela as a library of studies

## Context

The Capela was a study library on Catholic spirituality, theology and
philosophy. With Estudos planned as the home of all of Gustavo's studies, the
Capela can be what a chapel is for.

## Decision

When it reopens, the Capela is for **contemplating and praying**, with no
studies: an animated landing with music, candles of intention and letters
([product/chapel-contemplation.md](../../product/chapel-contemplation.md)). Its
current studies move to Estudos. **Intentions are private**: the public sees
only lit candles; only Gustavo reads the text. It stays closed
([0005](0005-spaces-under-construction.md)) until he resumes it.

## Why

Each space keeps one clear purpose (one verb); faith studies belong with the
other studies.

## Consequences

The current Capela code stays as the closed version until the rebuild.
Intentions are sensitive data under the LGPD (faith, health) and need spam
protection.
