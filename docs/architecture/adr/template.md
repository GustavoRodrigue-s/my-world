# NNNN — Title in the imperative or as a statement

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN | rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Supersedes:** —

## Context

What situation forces a decision? What constraints matter?

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly.

## Why

Why this option won. Keep it separate from the decision: the decision tells the
future what to do, the why preserves the reasoning.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option** — why not.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what must now be done. Files and
packages affected.
